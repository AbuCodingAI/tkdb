## The checklist

1. Who is making the claim, and what do they gain?
2. How was it measured, and on what sample?
3. How large is the effect, not just whether it exists?
4. Has anyone else reproduced it?

## Why effect size matters

A tiny effect can be statistically significant and still unimportant. Always ask "how much?".
