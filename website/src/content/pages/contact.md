title: Contact
description: How to get in touch with TKDB.
---

Questions, corrections, or feedback on a post? Email us at [tkdb_help@outlook.com](mailto:tkdb_help@outlook.com).

We read everything, but replies can take a few days. If you are reporting an error in an article, please include the link to the post so we can fix it quickly.

This site has no contact form and does not collect messages itself.
