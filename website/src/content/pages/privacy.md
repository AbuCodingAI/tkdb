---
title: Privacy
description: What TKDB does and does not collect.
---

TKDB is a static website.

- **No analytics, no ads and no tracking cookies** are active.
- **Local storage, not cookies.** Your theme (light or dark) and your saved or pinned posts are stored in your browser's local storage only. They are never sent anywhere, and you can clear them in your browser settings.
- **YouTube videos** do not load until you press play. Then they load from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode, and YouTube's own policy applies.
- **Hosting.** The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address and page requests.
- **Email.** If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

If analytics, advertising or comments are added in the future, this page will be updated first, and we will ask for your consent where the law requires it.
