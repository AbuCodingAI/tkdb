---
title: Privacy
description: What TKDB does and does not collect.
---

TKDB is a static website.

- **No analytics, no ads and no tracking cookies** are active.
- **Theme preference** (light or dark) is saved in your browser's local storage only. It is never sent anywhere.
- **YouTube videos** do not load until you press play. Then they load from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode, and YouTube's own policy applies.
- The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

If analytics, advertising or comments are added in the future, this page will be updated first.
