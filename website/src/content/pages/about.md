TKDB, The Kernel Daily Blog, analyzes the kernels of today: the core ideas behind technology and science.

## The creator

I'm Abu, the person behind TKDB. I'm a Linux user and builder: I started on Ubuntu this March, switched to Arch last month, and I've spent a lot of that time customizing XFCE into a macOS-style desktop. I also run a small network of projects at [hifiintec.com](https://hifiintec.com).

## Who writes what

**The news articles on TKDB are fully AI generated.** The daily science and technology stories are researched, written and fact-checked by an AI system, and every one is labelled that way. I don't write them.

**The Linux Internals blog is written by me.** Those posts, and the videos that start in November, are my own work. TKDB is independent: I make it, and I decide what goes on it. If you spot a mistake, please tell me.
